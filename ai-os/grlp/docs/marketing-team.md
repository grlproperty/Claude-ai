## Weekly rhythm (from the Brand Kit)
Monday new-listing launch · Tuesday education · Wednesday mid-week push and WhatsApp alert · Thursday lifestyle and brand · Friday open-day announcement · Saturday open-day coverage · Sunday weekly wrap-up.

## Approval process
Agent drafts → Brand Consistency Checker → approval queue → Mandy or Kandy approves or rejects with a reason → scheduled. Playlist or Brittany fonts, and any logo use outside the two approved versions, always go to management.
