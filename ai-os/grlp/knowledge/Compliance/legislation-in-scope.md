# Legislation the agency works under

This is a list for orientation, not legal advice. The system produces checklists of what is recorded; it never certifies compliance. Legal interpretation and regulatory submissions are reviewed by an authorised person.

| Law | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| Property Practitioners Act 22 of 2019 and PPRA requirements | Fidelity Fund Certificates; the mandatory disclosure form |
| Financial Intelligence Centre Act 38 of 2001 (FICA) | Estate agencies are accountable institutions; client identification documents |
| Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) | How client information is collected, used, shared and kept; direct marketing consent |
| Rental Housing Act 50 of 1999 | Deposits, interest on deposits, inspections and refunds |
| Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 | Lease terms; sellers acting in the ordinary course of business |
| Alienation of Land Act 68 of 1981 | Formalities for sale agreements; cooling-off on low-value sales |
| Prevention of Illegal Eviction Act 19 of 1998 | Eviction — never a step the system takes or advises on |

## Before any listing is marketed
The system checks that these are recorded: a signed mandate, the seller's mandatory disclosure form, and the seller's FICA documents.
