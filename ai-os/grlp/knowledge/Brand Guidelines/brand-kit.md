# Brand kit — colours, typography and logo

"Consistency builds trust. Clarity builds connection."

## Brand values
1. **Professionalism** — every touchpoint communicates authority and competence.
2. **Clarity** — straightforward, easy to read, free of embellishment. Simplicity is strength.
3. **Approachability** — warm, people-focused and community-driven.
4. **Integrity** — accuracy, honesty and respect. Properties and people are represented truthfully, always.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Maroon | #991C1F (RGB 164, 46, 43 · CMYK 0, 96, 80, 47 · Pantone ~188 C) | Logos, headings, accents, calls to action |
| White | #FFFFFF | Primary background |
| Light Grey | #F2F2F0 | Backgrounds |
| Mid Grey | #D5D5D0 | Borders |
| Charcoal | #3A3A3A | Body text |
| Charcoal Light | #5A5A5A | Secondary text |

Avoid bright or overly saturated colours.

## Typography
- **Montserrat** (Light 300 to Bold 700) for all headings and body copy.
- Approved display fonts: Montserrat, Montserrat Classic. **Playlist and Brittany only with management approval.**
- Align text left — never centred in body copy. Avoid excessive bold, italic or underlining. Clean, minimal layouts with white space.

## Logo
- Version 1: maroon on white — light backgrounds, preferred for print.
- Version 2: white on maroon — dark or maroon backgrounds, good for digital.
- Vertical/square version — social profiles, stamps, compact spaces.
- Clear space equal to the height of the "G" in GARDEN on every side.
- Do not stretch, crop or recolour. Only the two approved colour versions unless management approves.
- The system never draws the logo. It leaves a marked slot and a person places the approved file.

## Design standards
Minimal, uncluttered layouts. High-quality, well-lit, recent, honest photographs; no heavy filters. Every flyer carries the logo, contact details and the property reference number.
