# Tone and voice

**Do:** write professionally yet personally; use plain language and short sentences; let the property speak for itself; use South African spelling; proofread; be engaging and clear; keep formatting consistent.

**Do not:** use flowery or exaggerated language; overuse adjectives; put a comma before the final "and" in a list; use jargon; embellish or misrepresent a property; publish without proofreading.

**Format:** body text left-aligned; logo, contact and reference number on all flyers; consistent address and call-to-action placement; approved colours and fonts only; minimal, uncluttered designs; high-quality, well-lit photographs only.

## Example of an on-brand listing description
"This exceptional home offers generous living spaces, a sun-drenched garden and sweeping views of the Garden Route coastline. Set within a secure estate, it combines privacy with community in perfect measure. Contact us today to arrange a viewing."
