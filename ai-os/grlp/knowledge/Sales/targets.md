# Sales targets

Initial benchmarks. Management can change them at any time in the command centre under Targets, without any rebuild.

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Closed sales | 2 per agent per month |
| Average sale price | R 2 200 000 |
| Exclusive mandates | 4 per month |
| Valuation meetings | 8 per month |
| Prospecting contacts | 80 per month |

**Assumption to confirm:** the mandate, valuation and prospecting targets are treated as per agent, like the sales target, because together they form one activity ladder (80 contacts → 8 valuations → 4 mandates → 2 sales).

**Commission rate is deliberately not set.** Commission generated and forecast show as "rate not set" until management enters a rate, or commission is captured per sale.

The engine calculates daily, weekly and monthly prospecting, valuations booked, mandates secured, mandate conversion, viewings, offers, sales concluded and commission, per agent and for the company.
