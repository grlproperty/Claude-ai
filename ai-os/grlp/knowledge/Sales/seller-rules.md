# What the system may and may not do with sellers

- No agent may make an unverified property valuation or promise a selling price. The system prepares information; a registered practitioner forms and gives the opinion.
- Comparable sales are tabulated only from figures a practitioner supplies, with the workings shown.
- Seller outreach and follow-up are drafts until the responsible agent approves them.
- Prospecting by electronic message needs a lawful basis under POPIA and an opt-out every time.
