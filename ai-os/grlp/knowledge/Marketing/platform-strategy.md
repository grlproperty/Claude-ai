# Social media platform strategy — post every day

| Platform | Role | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Community and listings; primary platform for George, Wilderness and Sedgefield | New listing posts daily, open-day events and reminders, sold and under-offer updates, market and area news, community stories |
| Instagram | Visual and lifestyle | Feed property photos daily, reels of walkthroughs and lifestyle, behind-the-scenes stories, before-and-after carousels, testimonial quote cards |
| TikTok | Video and reach | 15–60 second property tours, buying and selling tips, "Did you know?" area facts, agent personality, trending audio with listings |
| LinkedIn | Professional and B2B | Market statistics and insights, sold portfolio highlights, agent achievements, thought leadership, development opportunities |
| WhatsApp | Direct alerts to **opted-in buyers and sellers only** | New listing alerts, price reductions, open-day reminders, offer-accepted updates, personal follow-ups. Brief and purposeful; under 200 words; never over-communicate |

## Weekly schedule
- **Monday** (FB, IG, TT): new listing launch day — feature the best new mandate.
- **Tuesday** (FB, LI, TT): educational content — market tips, area guide, buying and selling advice.
- **Wednesday** (FB, IG, WA): mid-week listing push — WhatsApp alert for new or reduced properties.
- **Thursday** (IG, TT, LI): lifestyle and brand — scenery, agent spotlight, testimonial.
- **Friday** (FB, IG, WA): open-day announcement for the weekend.
- **Saturday** (FB, IG, TT): open-day live coverage.
- **Sunday** (FB, LI, IG): weekly wrap-up — sold highlights, statistics, the week ahead.

## Content pillars
45% listings · 25% lifestyle · 20% education · 10% brand and team.

## Caption formula (every post)
1. **Hook** — the best feature or an engaging question.
2. **Details** — beds · baths · size · price · mandate type.
3. **Lifestyle** — the feeling: coastal, private, investment.
4. **CTA** — contact [agent] on [number] or link in bio.
5. **Hashtags** — 8 to 10 from the approved list.
