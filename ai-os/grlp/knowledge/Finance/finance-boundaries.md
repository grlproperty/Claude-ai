# Finance — boundaries

Finance agents are read-only. They produce dashboards for revenue, sales commission, rental income, operating expenses, outstanding payments, cash flow, budgets and forecasts from entries captured by authorised staff.

Every payment, transfer, accounting submission and financial commitment requires human authorisation. The figures are management information, not accounting records.
