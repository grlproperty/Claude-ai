# Rentals and property management — boundaries

The system tracks landlords, tenants, properties, leases, renewals, maintenance, payment information and outstanding actions.

It does **not** approve tenants, change leases, initiate payments or make legal decisions. Those stay with the rentals agent and the principal.

- Leases are flagged 90 days before they end so the renewal conversation starts early.
- Maintenance is classified as urgent (respond today), high (one working day) or routine (three working days). The contractor and the spend are a person's decision.
- All client money moves through the agency trust account, handled by authorised people only.
