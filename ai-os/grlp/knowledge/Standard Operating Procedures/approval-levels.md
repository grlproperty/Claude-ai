# Approval levels — green, amber, red

**Green — automatic.** Research organisation, drafts, internal summaries, task creation, data analysis, internal reporting.

**Amber — a person reviews first.** Social media content, website updates, client communications, marketing campaigns, financial reports, customer-facing documents.

**Red — explicit, typed authorisation.** Sending money, changing bank details, signing agreements, entering contracts, legal commitments, legally significant communications, deleting important records, changing access permissions, employment decisions. For GRLP also: approving a tenant, changing a lease, issuing a valuation, changing a price, and regulatory submissions.

An agent can never bypass an approval by handing the work to another agent: the level belongs to the action, not to who asks.
