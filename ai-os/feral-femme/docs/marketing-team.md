# Feral Femme — the one-person marketing team

The marketing model, mapped to the registry. Roles that already exist are mapped, not duplicated. **Bold** = live today.

**Strategy in one line:** help readers find verified work. Every post points to a note, a tool or a source. No advertising, no sponsorship, no influencer tactics, no urgency. The Editorial Director approves everything.

## Rhythm
Publication-led, not calendar-led: a note is published when it is verified, and the briefing and social posts follow it. Between notes: one tool, one archive frame with its disclosure, or one "how to check this yourself" post.

## Approval process
Agent drafts → voice check → approval queue → the Editorial Director approves or rejects with a reason. A caption uses only the approved title, summary and source names. Any reply to criticism or a correction request goes to a person.
