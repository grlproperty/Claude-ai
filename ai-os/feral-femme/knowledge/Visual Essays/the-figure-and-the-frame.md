# The figure and the frame

## The figure
One recurring figure appears in every frame. She carries the marks of both subjects at once, and she looks straight back at whoever is looking.

1. **Fox ears** — prosthetic, real fur tones, always worn. Never a costume headband.
2. **The blaze** — a white stripe painted from brow to nose.
3. **Painted muzzle** — darkened nose tip and a line to the lip.
4. **Smudged eye** — dark, slept-in shadow. Never clean glamour make-up.
5. **One restraint** — a collar, wire, thread or line. A single detail, not a set.
6. **The stare** — direct and unsmiling. She is not asking to be liked.

## The frame
- **The studio** — a plain ground, one or two figures, one idea. Where the argument is made clearly.
- **The street** — protest, crowd, megaphone, hand-painted slogans, the sticker on the lamp post. Where the argument goes public.
- **The scene** — the industry itself as a set: the field, the wardrobe, the fur, the aviary. One documented case per frame.

**Direction:** hard flash or plain daylight. Real texture: plaster, tile, fur, skin. Portrait 4:5 or 3:4. Crimson enters as an object in the scene, never as a wash over the picture.

**Retired from the 2024 kit:** the caged-collage look — repeated caged figures in red frames on pink, soft-focus chains, cool desaturated grading. A cage may appear in a frame. It is no longer the whole idea.

## The archive frame — one case per frame
Every post in the visual essay series carries a single documented case: one sentence, one image, one source.

1. Wordmark top left. Series or section stamp top right, in mono.
2. Headline in Cormorant. The turn of the sentence sits in a crimson highlight.
3. One image: the figure, or an engraving-style drawing of the animal.
4. Source and handle at the foot. **A frame without a source is not published.**

## How the frames are made
The visual essays are created with AI image generation, directed by Ayden Rosemary Brown. Each is conceptual editorial work, not documentary photography. Say so wherever the images travel.
