# Visual essays and imagery disclosure

The archive's visual essays are AI-generated, directed by the founder, and conceptual rather than documentary. This is disclosed on the archive and on the About page.

- No image is presented as photographic evidence of a specific event, and none should be read as such.
- Generated or conceptual imagery is always clearly distinguished from documentary photographs.
- Every visual has a documented source, case reference or editorial explanation.
- No likeness of a real person, no company logo, no trade dress.

**Standard disclosure line:** "AI-generated conceptual editorial imagery, directed by Ayden Rosemary Brown. Not documentary photography, and not evidence of any specific event."
