# How an investigation moves

Research → Verification → Editorial draft → Review → Visual production → Approval → Website publication → Newsletter distribution → Social distribution → Archive.

There are four human gates:
1. **Verification sheet** — a person opens each source and confirms it says what the claim says.
2. **Editorial review** — framing, credit and tone.
3. **Final approval to publish** — red, with typed authorisation, whenever the note contains an allegation about a named party.
4. **Social posts.**

Automated checks confirm structure only: that sources are complete and dated, that claims are classified, that every figure in the draft is on the verification sheet, that every publisher is credited, and that the voice is restrained.
