# Editorial constitution

**Two subjects. One machine. Same refusal to consent.**

Feral Femme investigates every industry built on a body that could not agree to it — and holds animals and women at equal weight. Neither is a metaphor for the other. Both are literal.

**Standard:** Written to be verified, not agreed with.
**Motto:** Knowledge Creates Change.
**Descriptor:** Editorial · Research · Tools. Established 2026. South Africa. Self-funded.
**Founder and Editorial Director:** Ayden Rosemary Brown.

## The ten industries
Beauty · Fashion · Food & Agriculture · Animal Welfare · Women's Rights · Environment · Technology · Consumer Rights · Corporate Accountability · Media & Advertising.

## Method
1. **Public-record reporting.** Field notes summarise investigations that already exist in the public record. We summarise; we do not scoop.
2. **Editorial synthesis.** Each source is read, reframed in a restrained editorial voice and connected to the pattern of its industry. The original publisher always gets the credit.
3. **AI-directed editorial imagery.** The archive's visual essays are AI-generated, directed by the founder, conceptual rather than documentary.
4. **Free tools.** Interactive tools turn awareness into practice. No account, no sign-up, no payment.

## Independence
No advertisers. No industry sponsorship. No affiliate links. No sponsored placements. No funding from any company whose conduct the platform documents. Feral Femme is funded by its readers: donations, and the documents it sells.

## What Feral Femme is not
Not a lifestyle blog, not an influencer platform, not an advertising-supported publication. No agent may propose turning it into one.
