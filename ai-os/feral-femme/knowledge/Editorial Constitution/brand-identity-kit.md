# Brand identity kit — Edition 2026

**What it is:** an editorial platform. Field notes, a numbered visual archive, a public reading list, ten free tools and four free guides.
**What it does:** translates the vocabulary, cites the source, and files the receipt. Every claim is traceable to a named publisher, regulator or research body.
**Who pays for it:** its readers, and nobody else. No advertisers, no industry sponsorship, no affiliate revenue.

Retired from the 2024 kit: "Exposing animal cruelty in beauty" and "educational activism platform". The remit is ten industries and two subjects, and the platform is editorial: it documents and cites.

## Wordmark — two words and a full stop
- **FERAL FEMME.** in Bodoni Moda Medium, uppercase. FERAL in crimson, FEMME in ink, the full stop in crimson. The stop is part of the name.
- Versions: primary stacked; horizontal; on crimson (the whole mark is white); avatar "FF."
- Tracking: stacked at display size −0.015em, line-height 0.86. Horizontal at small size +0.26em, one line.
- Clear space: the height of the capital F on every side. Nothing enters it, including image edges.
- Grounds: blush, white or crimson. On an image, set it on a clear, light area.
- Not this: on black; without the full stop; with the colours swapped; redrawn in script, brush or graffiti lettering.
- The system never redraws the wordmark. It leaves a slot and a person places the approved file.

## Colour — blush carries it, crimson signs it
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Blush | #F6DFE2 | The ground: pages, posts, covers |
| Crimson | #8E0B14 | The signature: marks, labels, bands, buttons |
| Ink | #12100F | Text and symbols only. Never a ground |
| White | #FDFCFC | The surface: cards, forms, documents |
| Blush Pale | #F9E7EA | Alternate panels and bands |
| Blush Deep | #EFD8DC | Pressed states, inset fields |
| Crimson Deep | #6E1220 | Hover on crimson |
| Dim | #4A3C3E | Secondary text, captions |

Proportion: about 60 blush, 25 crimson, 10 ink, 5 white. Approved pairings: ink on blush, ink on white, crimson on blush, crimson on white, white on crimson, blush on crimson.

Colour is flat. No gradients, no tints of crimson, no additional hues. Rules are crimson at 32% or ink at 14%. Depth comes from a hard offset shadow, never a blur.

## Type — four faces, four jobs
| Job | Face | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Bodoni Moda, Medium 500, uppercase | The name, section titles, numerals |
| Editorial | Cormorant Garamond, Semibold 600 headlines; Light 300 to Medium 500 ledes and claims; sentence case | Headlines, ledes, claims |
| Reading and interface | Jost, Light 300 body at 16 on 28; Medium 500 uppercase +0.16em for navigation and buttons | Body, navigation, buttons |
| Evidence | Space Mono, Regular and Bold, uppercase +0.1em | Labels, dates, counts, sources, stamps |

Montserrat and the two-family limit from the 2024 kit are retired. Hand-lettered and graffiti type appears inside images only, never as set type.

## Components — square, flat, sourced
Corners are square. Rules are hairline. Shadows are a hard offset in crimson at 13 to 20%, or ink at 14%.

## The documents
Free guides sit on blush. Paid workbooks sit on crimson. Same grid, same numeral, same foot.

## Do
Lead with blush and let crimson sign · set the name in Bodoni Moda, with its full stop · put a named source beside every claim · keep corners square and rules hairline · use the figure: ears, blaze, muzzle, stare · disclose that the frames are AI-directed editorial work · give each frame one idea and room around it.

## Don't
Use ink or black as a ground · add gradients, blurs, rounded corners or new colours · set type in script, brush or graffiti faces · use stock photography, cute animals or gore · present a staged frame as documentary · use emoji, exclamation marks or urgency · bring back the 2024 marks: the FF circle, the round stamp, Montserrat.
