# Field note format

Field notes are Markdown files in `content/field-notes/`, ordered by a numeric filename prefix that never appears in the address.

Front matter: `title`, `summary` (one or two sentences that stand alone), `topic`, `topics`, `form` ("Short" or "Long read"), `duration` (minutes), `date` (publication date) and `order`.

`date` is not decorative: it is what the feed publishes, and it is how the newsletter decides what is new. A note without one is invisible to both.
