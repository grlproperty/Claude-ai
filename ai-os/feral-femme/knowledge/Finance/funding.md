# Funding and finance

- **Donations:** PayPal, charged in USD. Tiers start at $5 (Supporter) and $20 (Contributor), with an any-amount option. Donors may name a cause: General Research Fund, Animal Testing Investigation, Fast Fashion Reporting, Food Systems Research, Greenwashing Coverage, Tech & Labour Research.
- **Documents:** four paid documents at $30 (see the product catalogue).
- A donation funds independent investigative work with no strings attached.

Finance agents are read-only. They never pay, refund or change payment details. Every payment and financial commitment is the founder's.
