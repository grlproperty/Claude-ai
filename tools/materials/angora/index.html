Angora

animal

What it is

Extremely soft fibre from the Angora rabbit (distinct from Angora goat mohair).

Animal welfare

Undercover footage of live-plucking of rabbits led most major retailers to ban angora. The welfare risk is high and hard to verify.

Environment

Low volume; impacts dominated by the welfare issue rather than land or water.

Alternatives

Brushed cotton, recycled fibres, and soft synthetics or regenerated cellulosics for the same halo/softness.

Source — PETA angora investigations; retailer bans (2013 onward).
