Record Checker

Documented regulatory actions, court rulings, and formal findings, each tied to a named public source. This holds only what the public record shows. Absence of a record is not evidence of good practice.

JBS

Food / meat

Documented finding

NY Attorney General sued JBS USA (Feb 2024) over its ‘Net Zero by 2040’ marketing; separately paid US$4M in fines (Jan 2025) over child-labour violations at US facilities.

Source — NY Attorney General; US Dept. of Labor. · Open the record

TotalEnergies

Energy / advertising

Documented finding

Paris Judicial Tribunal ruled (Oct 2025) that its ‘carbon neutrality by 2050’ marketing breached consumer-protection law — a first against a major oil company for greenwashing.

Source — Paris Judicial Tribunal. · Open the record

Shein

Fashion

Documented finding

Italy’s competition authority fined a Shein-linked company €1M (Aug 2025) for misleading environmental claims about its clothing.

Source — AGCM (Italian Competition Authority). · Open the record

KLM

Aviation / advertising

Documented finding

Amsterdam District Court ruled (2024) that KLM’s ‘Fly Responsibly’ carbon-offset claims lacked adequate, verifiable evidence.

Source — Amsterdam District Court. · Open the record

L’Oréal

Beauty

Documented finding

Excluded from the Leaping Bunny programme; the company states it tests ‘where required by law’, a reference to markets such as China where animal testing can be required.

Source — Cruelty Free International. · Open the record

Boohoo

Fashion

Documented finding

UK reporting and an independent review (Levitt QC, 2020) found serious supply-chain failings in Leicester garment factories, including underpayment; 50+ MPs wrote to the Home Secretary.

Source — Levitt QC independent review; UK Parliament. · Open the record

Mobilewalla

Tech / data

Documented finding

US FTC finalised an order (Jan 2025) banning the data broker from selling sensitive location data, including visits to health facilities and places of worship.

Source — US Federal Trade Commission. · Open the record

Gravy Analytics / Venntel

Tech / data

Documented finding

US FTC action (Dec 2024) banning the sale of sensitive location data; the companies processed billions of location signals daily from ~1 billion devices.

Source — US Federal Trade Commission. · Open the record

H&M

Fashion / fast fashion

Documented finding

EU regulators (2022) found the vast majority of H&M’s sustainability claims unsubstantiated; H&M reached a US$3M settlement over its “Conscious Collection” marketing and removed Higg sustainability scores after they were challenged.

Source — EU consumer authorities; US class-action settlement · Open the record

Zara / Inditex

Fashion / fast fashion

Documented finding

Remains within the EU Consumer Protection Cooperation Network’s review scope; watchdogs flagged product terms (‘natural’, ‘sustainable fabric’) now restricted under EU rules. No formal EU sanction published as of 2026 — scrutiny ongoing.

Source — EU CPC Network; Business & Human Rights Resource Centre · Open the record

Nike

Fashion / footwear

Documented finding

Named in a 2025 report (The Missing Thread) for bold decarbonisation pledges that omit protections for supply-chain workers affected by the shift — ‘greenwashing’ by exclusion of labour.

Source — Business & Human Rights Resource Centre · Open the record

Sunday Riley

Beauty / skincare

Documented finding

Settled with the US FTC over fake reviews — the CEO and staff posted reviews of their own products on Sephora.com and used a VPN to evade detection. Settlement reached; no admission of fault.

Source — US FTC settlement (2019) · Open the record

Grande Cosmetics

Beauty / lash & brow

Documented finding

Agreed to a US$6.25M class-action settlement (Jan 2024) over claims it failed to disclose that its serums contain isopropyl cloprostenate, alleged to carry undisclosed side-effect risks. Company maintains products are safe.

Source — Class-action settlement (2024) · Open the record

Sephora

Beauty / retail

Documented finding

Agreed to pay US$775,000 (2025) to settle California claims over improper hazardous-waste disposal. Note: a separate suit alleging its ‘Clean at Sephora’ label was misleading was dismissed in 2024 — the court found no deception.

Source — California settlement (2025); NY federal court dismissal (2024) · Open the record

Yves Rocher

Beauty / cosmetics

Documented finding

Under examination by Paris judges over France’s corporate duty-of-vigilance law, concerning alleged labour violations in Turkey. A ruling is expected in 2026 — this is a pending case, not a finding.

Source — French duty-of-vigilance proceedings (ruling expected 2026) · Open the record

Coca-Cola, Nestlé, Kraft Heinz & others

Food / ultra-processed

Documented finding

Named in a San Francisco lawsuit (Dec 2025) alleging deceptive marketing of ultra-processed foods, including health-oriented messaging and child-directed branding, under California’s Unfair Competition Law. Pending — allegations, not proven; companies dispute the claims.

Source — City of San Francisco lawsuit (2025), pending · Open the record

Kroger

Food / retail

Documented finding

Subject of a 2024 class action alleging eggs labelled ‘Farm Fresh’ came from chickens in industrial confinement. A documented labelling dispute — consult the case for the current status.

Source — Sorkin v. Kroger Co. (N.D. Ill. 2024) · Open the record

Walmart

Food / retail

Documented finding

Named in a 2024 case alleging salmon marketed as ‘sustainable’ was deceptively labelled. A documented labelling dispute — consult the case for the current status.

Source — Sanchez v. Walmart Inc. (N.D. Ill. 2024) · Open the record

Canada Goose

Fashion / outerwear (animals)

Documented finding

After a PETA complaint to the US FTC, the company stopped making claims that its standards ensured suppliers didn’t abuse animals. It pledged to end fur use (2021/2022) but faced continued campaigns over down sourcing. Mix of regulatory outcome + ongoing advocacy scrutiny.

Source — US FTC complaint outcome; PETA campaigns · Open the record

Patagonia

Fashion / outerwear (animals)

Documented finding

Twice suspended wool purchasing after PETA exposés documented cruelty at approved ‘responsible wool’ suppliers that violated the brand’s own welfare standards. Advocacy-sourced investigation; the brand has responded and changed suppliers.

Source — PETA investigations (advocacy source) · Open the record

Estée Lauder

Beauty / cosmetics

Documented finding

A US federal judge (SDNY) declined to dismiss a securities-fraud suit alleging the company concealed its reliance on ‘daigou’ gray-market sales in China, later reaching a settlement with investors. Investor-disclosure matter, not a product claim.

Source — SDNY securities litigation; investor settlement · Open the record
