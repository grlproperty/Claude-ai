Regenerative Organic Certified (ROC)

environment

Run by Regenerative Organic Alliance

What it verifies

Builds on organic certification with added requirements for soil health, animal welfare, and farmworker fairness — one of the stricter farm-level marks.

What it does not guarantee

Still farm-level: it certifies how a raw material was grown, not how the final product was manufactured or by whom.

Source — Regenerative Organic Alliance.
