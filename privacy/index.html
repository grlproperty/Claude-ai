Last updated: 31 August 2026

FERAL FEMME is operated from South Africa and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

This site is built as static files. It has no database, no user accounts, and no server-side processing of visitor data.

What we do not do

No cookies. This site sets no cookies of any kind — not for analytics, not for preferences, not for advertising.

No third-party fonts. Bodoni Moda, Cormorant Garamond, and Jost are served from this domain. Requesting a font from a third party would disclose your IP address and the page you are reading to that provider on every visit.

No third-party scripts, embeds, or trackers. No social widgets, no embedded players, no tag managers, no advertising pixels. Every asset on every page is served from this domain.

No profiling, no cross-site tracking, and no personal information sold or shared with anyone.

Analytics

A previous version of this site loaded Google Analytics. This build loads no analytics script at all.

If analytics are ever enabled, they will be cookieless and aggregate-only, from a provider that does not track visitors across sites, and that provider will be named on this page before it is switched on.

What is unavoidable

Our hosting provider records standard server logs — IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent — as an inherent part of delivering a web page. These are retained by the provider under its own policy and are not used by us to build profiles or identify individuals.

The briefing

If you subscribe to The Research Briefing, we hold your email address for the sole purpose of sending it. It is used for nothing else, shared with no one, and deleted on unsubscribe. Every issue carries a one-click unsubscribe link.

Donations

Donations are processed by PayPal. We do not receive or store card details. PayPal holds payment data under its own privacy policy, which applies at the point of payment. If you give a name and email for a receipt, they are used to send that receipt and to acknowledge the donation.

Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject you have the right to be notified that your personal information is being collected, to access it, to request correction or deletion, to object to processing, and to complain to the Information Regulator.

In practice the only personal information we hold about a reader is a briefing email address, if you have given us one.

To exercise any right, or to raise a concern, write to info@feral-femme.co.

You may also complain directly to the Information Regulator (South Africa), which supervises compliance with POPIA.

Readers outside South Africa

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies to you, the equivalent rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection are honoured on the same terms and through the same address.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the date at the top of this page. We do not apply changes retroactively to information already collected.

Self-funded

Feral Femme is self-funded and reader-supported. A donation funds independent investigative work with no strings attached — no advertisers, no industry sponsorship, no editorial conflicts of interest.
