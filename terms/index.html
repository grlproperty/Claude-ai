Nature of the content

FERAL FEMME publishes editorial and research material about industry conduct across ten sectors.

It is not legal advice, financial advice, or professional advice of any kind. It is not a certification, an endorsement, or a statement about the current compliance of any company or product. Standards change and companies change; entries reflect the position at the date stated on the page.

Field notes summarise reporting that already exists in the public record. Where a note describes an investigation, the finding belongs to the original publisher, who is credited in the note, and any characterisation of it is ours.

Named companies

The Record Checker and the field notes name companies in connection with documented regulatory actions, court rulings, and formal findings, each tied to a named public source.

These entries describe matters of public record. They are not allegations made by this platform. Where a company disputes an entry, or where a matter has been overturned, withdrawn, or superseded, write to corrections@feral-femme.co and we will review it and, where warranted, correct or remove it and record the change at /corrections/.

Absence from the record is not evidence of good practice, and is not presented as such anywhere on this platform.

Accuracy

We take considerable care with accuracy, and our editorial standards set out how. We do not warrant that every statement is free from error, and we correct errors promptly when identified.
