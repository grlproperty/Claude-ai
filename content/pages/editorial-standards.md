These standards apply to every entry published on this platform. They are published so that a reader can check whether we have met them.

## Sourcing

**Primary sources first.** Every factual claim about a legal position is sourced to the instrument itself — the regulation, statute, rule, or decision — not to secondary coverage of it. Every claim about a test method is sourced to the adopted test guideline or the validation report.

**Secondary sources are labelled.** Where we rely on an NGO's compilation, an industry statement, or press reporting, we say so and name it. These sources are useful and frequently accurate; they are not primary, and the distinction is preserved.

**Links go to the source.** Citations link to the instrument where a stable public URL exists. Where one does not, the citation gives enough detail to locate the document.

## Numbers

We do not publish a statistic we cannot source to a named body and a dated publication.

This rules out a great deal of widely circulated material. Global figures for animals used in cosmetics testing are frequently quoted without traceable derivation, and estimates that cannot be checked are not published here, however favourable to the argument.

Where a figure exists but is contested or its basis is unclear, we give the figure, name its source, and describe the limitation.

## Uncertainty

Where the evidence does not support a clean conclusion, the entry says so. Where two credible bodies read the same instrument differently — as certification schemes did after China's 2021 reform — we describe both readings rather than adopting one silently.

Where a scientific gap exists, we state its extent. The credibility of everything else on this platform depends on this being done consistently, including when it is inconvenient.

## Review

Entries carry a publication date and, where applicable, a date of last review.

Datasets — the regulation tracker, the methods index, the certification guide — carry a declared review cycle. When a dataset passes its cycle without review, the page says so on its face rather than ageing quietly. That notice is generated automatically at build time from the data file's own metadata; it is not something an editor has to remember to add.

## Corrections

We correct errors of fact promptly and visibly.

**Substantive corrections** — where an error changes the meaning of an entry — are noted on the entry itself and listed at [/corrections/](/corrections/) with the date, the error, and the correction.

**Minor corrections** — typography, broken links, formatting — are made without notice.

**We do not silently delete.** An entry that is withdrawn is marked as withdrawn with an explanation, and its URL continues to resolve.

To report an error, write to [corrections@feral-femme.co](mailto:corrections@feral-femme.co) with the entry, the passage, and the basis for the correction.

## Independence

We accept no funding from cosmetics manufacturers, retailers, ingredient suppliers, contract research organisations, or certification bodies.

Where research is commissioned, the funder is named on the resulting publication, and the commissioning agreement gives the funder no right of approval over findings. Full detail is at [/funding/](/funding/).

No entry on this platform is written, reviewed, or approved by any party outside the platform.

## AI use

Language models are used in preparing this platform — for drafting, structuring, and checking internal consistency. They are not used as a source of fact.

Every factual claim, citation, date, and legal reference is verified against the primary document by a human before publication. A model's assertion that a regulation says something is not evidence that it does, and is never treated as such.

We state this because the alternative — using these tools and not saying so — is the practice this platform exists to argue against in other contexts.
