## The work

### What is FERAL FEMME?

An independent editorial and research platform documenting the industries built on a body that could not agree to it — holding animals and women at equal weight. Neither is a metaphor for the other. Both are literal.

### Is it free?

The reading is. Every field note, every decoder, the archive, the reading list and the tools are free, with no account, no sign-up and no payment. Six documents are sold, and two of those have a free printable card taken from them so you can read the work before paying for any of it.

### Who pays for it?

Nobody but readers. There are no advertisers, no industry sponsorship, no affiliate links and no sponsored placements. It runs on the documents and on direct donations, which is the whole reason both exist.

### Do you take money from the industries you write about?

No. Not as advertising, not as sponsorship, not as gifted product, not as affiliate commission. If that ever changes it will be declared on the disclosures page before anything is published, not after.

## The sources

### Where does the reporting come from?

The public record. Field notes summarise investigations that already exist — regulatory rulings, court filings, company disclosures, peer-reviewed studies, parliamentary inquiries and the work of named news organisations — read in full and connected to the pattern of the industry they document. The original publisher always gets the credit.

### Do you do original reporting?

The editorial synthesis is original; the underlying findings are not, and are never presented as if they were. What this site adds is the reading, the reframing, the connection between cases, and the standard every claim has to meet before it is published.

### What is that standard?

A finding is publishable when a named source, dated and retrievable, states it — and when the sentence written here does not claim more than that source does. Where something could not be established, it is recorded as not established rather than left out.

### Can I check a claim myself?

That is the intention. Every note links its sources to the original publisher, and the reading list and the sources page name every register, database and authority the method draws on. Nothing here asks to be taken on trust.

## Corrections

### What happens if something here is wrong?

Write to us. Substantive corrections are published in full on the corrections page, in the same place and at the same size as the original — never edited in silently. A withdrawn note keeps its address and is marked as withdrawn rather than deleted.

### Do you contact companies before publishing?

Where a note makes a specific allegation about a named company, yes, and the response — or the fact that there was none, with the date of the approach — is published with it. Silence is recorded as silence, not as an admission.

## The documents

### What am I buying?

A PDF. Each one lists its page count, its contents and its licence on the shop page before you pay. The two professional packs carry a single-organisation licence, which means you can circulate them internally as widely as you like; the reader volumes are for personal use.

### How does it arrive?

By email, from a person — not as a download from this site. PayPal asks for your address at checkout and that is where it goes, usually within a few hours and always within one working day. Use an address you can actually receive at, and if it goes to the wrong one, reply to the PayPal receipt and it gets fixed rather than lost.

### Can I get a refund?

A delivered file cannot be un-received, so payment for one is final. There are four exceptions and they are not grudging: it never arrives, the wrong file arrives, it will not open, or you paid twice. Any of those and it is replaced or refunded in full. None of this removes a statutory right to cancel where one applies, and PayPal's own buyer protection is separate from this policy.

### Do I get later editions?

Yes. Every future revision of a document is included in the purchase, and you are told when an edition changes.

### Can I quote or teach from this?

Quote freely with attribution and a link — that is what it is for. Reproducing a paid document in full, reselling it, or redistributing it outside the licence it was sold under is not covered. For classroom or newsroom use beyond that, write and ask; the answer is usually yes.

## Privacy and practicalities

### Do you track me?

No analytics, no advertising trackers, no third-party scripts, and no cookies. The site stores one thing in your browser — which currency you chose to read prices in — and that never leaves your device. The privacy page sets out how this sits with POPIA.

### What happens if I subscribe?

You get each field note in full on the day it is published, and nothing else. No promotions, no product recommendations, no sponsored placements. One click unsubscribes.

### How do I get in touch?

The contact page lists every route, and the find us page lists the platforms. For a correction, a licensing question, or a document that has not arrived, email is fastest and reaches a person.
