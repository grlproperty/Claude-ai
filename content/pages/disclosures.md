A platform that documents misleading claims cannot itself be ambiguous about how its own material is made. Everything a reader might reasonably want to check about this site is set out here.

## The imagery

The visual essays in the [archive](/archive/) are created using AI image generation, directed by Ayden Rosemary Brown.

Each image is a deliberate frame — conceptual editorial work, not documentary photography. **No image on this platform is presented as photographic evidence of a specific event, and none should be read as such.** Where a field note describes something that happened, the evidence for it is the cited source, never the picture beside it.

The research is not made this way. Field notes are written from the public record — named investigations, filings, rulings, and peer-reviewed work — and every one of them credits the publisher it draws on. The [sources list](/sources/) is standing and public.

## The money

FERAL FEMME is self-funded and reader-supported. Nobody has ever paid to appear here, and nobody can.

There are no advertisers, no industry sponsorship, and no affiliate links. Where the site sells something, what is sold and what the money does is set out in [Funding & Conflicts](/funding/).

## The data

The decoders, the record, the directory, and the reading list each declare when they were last reviewed and how often they should be. When one passes its review cycle the page says so on its own face rather than ageing quietly.

The Record Checker holds only what the public record shows, and states plainly that an absence of records is not evidence of good practice.

## Getting it wrong

Corrections are published, not quietly edited. Every one names the entry, the passage, and the basis for the change, and they are all listed at [/corrections/](/corrections/).

If something here is wrong, [tell us](/contact/) and it will be fixed in public.
