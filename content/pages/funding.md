FERAL FEMME is self-funded. Nobody has ever paid to appear here, and nobody can.

This page exists because we now sell things, and a research site that sells things owes its readers a plain account of where the money comes from — written before anyone has to ask for it, rather than after.

## What we sell

**Two documents.** [The Claims Compliance Pack and The Greenwashing Field Guide](/shop/). Both are assembled from the same reviewed datasets that power the free tools on this site. Nothing in either is withheld from the free version.

**A claim check.** [One page read for R3,500](/claim-check/): we tell a brand which of its claims hold and which do not. It is the same work published here, pointed at one product instead of the industry.

**Reader support.** [Donations](/donate/), which fund research time and hosting and buy nothing else.

**Commissioned research**, where an organisation funds a specific investigation. Where this happens, the funder is named on the piece, and we retain the right to publish what we find. If we have not published anything under this heading yet, it is because nobody has commissioned anything yet.

## Who we will not work with

We will not take money from any company documented in the [Record Checker](/tools/record/), in any form, ever. Twenty-one companies are named there against specific allegations. A payment from any of them — a commission, a sponsorship, a bulk order of the guides — would make every entry on this site negotiable.

We do not sell endorsements. There is nothing a company can buy that appears here as praise — not a mention, not a favourable entry, not the removal of an unfavourable one.

We do not run affiliate links. If we earned a commission on a product we had assessed, every assessment here would be worthless, including the honest ones and including the ones written years earlier. There is no fee that makes this worth doing.

We do not publish sponsored posts, and we do not accept payment to cover a subject or to leave one alone.

## The wall

Nothing found in a claim check is published without the client's agreement. That is a normal confidentiality term and clients are entitled to it.

The reverse holds too, and matters more: **buying something from us does not buy any influence over what is published here.** A company that has paid for a claim check, bought the compliance pack, funded a piece of research, or donated cannot have a field note changed, an entry softened, a certification's scope described more generously, or its own record removed.

If a funder's conduct becomes newsworthy, we will report it, and the funding will be disclosed in the piece.

If paid work ever puts us in a position where we cannot report something — because of what we learned under confidentiality, or what we agreed in order to do the work — we will say that a conflict exists and decline to cover the subject rather than cover it partially. Silence dressed as editorial judgement is the thing this whole site is about.

## Corrections

Corrections go to [info@feral-femme.co](mailto:info@feral-femme.co) and are published in full at [/corrections/](/corrections/), with what was said, what was wrong, and when it changed. A correction is never quietly edited in.

This applies to this page too. If our funding changes, this page changes first.
