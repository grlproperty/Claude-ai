*Last updated: 31 August 2026*

This site is built as static files. It has no database, no user accounts, and no server-side processing of visitor data.

## What we do not do

**No cookies.** This site sets no cookies of any kind — not for analytics, not for preferences, not for advertising.

**No third-party fonts.** Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat are served from this domain. Requesting a font from a third party would disclose your IP address and the page you are reading to that provider on every visit. On a platform about ethical clarity, that would be difficult to defend.

**No third-party scripts, embeds, or trackers.** No social media widgets, no embedded players, no tag managers, no advertising pixels. Every asset on every page is served from this domain.

**No fingerprinting**, no cross-site tracking, no data sold or shared with anyone.

## What is unavoidable

Our hosting provider records standard server logs — IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent — as an inherent part of delivering a web page. These are retained by the provider under their own policy and are not used by us to build profiles or identify individuals.

## Analytics

If analytics are enabled on this site, they will be cookieless and aggregate-only, from a provider that does not track visitors across sites and does not sell data. That would be disclosed here specifically, naming the provider, before it was switched on.

At the time of writing, no analytics script is loaded on any page.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to The Dispatch, we hold your email address for the purpose of sending it. It is used for nothing else, shared with no one, and deleted on unsubscribe. Every issue carries a one-click unsubscribe link. The provider we use to send it is named on the subscription page.

## Payments

Payments for supporting memberships are processed by a third-party payment provider. We do not receive or store card details. The provider holds payment data under its own privacy policy, which is linked at the point of payment.

## Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection in relation to personal data we hold. In practice the only personal data we hold about a reader is a newsletter email address, if you have given us one.

To exercise any right, write to [hello@feral-femme.co](mailto:hello@feral-femme.co).

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the date at the top of this page. We do not make changes retroactively applicable to data already collected.
