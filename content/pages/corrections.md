We correct errors of fact promptly and visibly. Substantive corrections — those that change the meaning of an entry — are recorded here permanently. Minor corrections to typography, links, and formatting are made without notice.

To report an error, write to [corrections@feral-femme.co](mailto:corrections@feral-femme.co) with the entry, the passage, and the basis for the correction. We would rather be corrected than quoted incorrectly.

## Record

No substantive corrections have been issued since the archive was published.

This entry is not left empty as an oversight. A corrections page with nothing on it either means no substantive errors have been reported, or means errors are not being recorded. We record them here, and this line will be replaced when the first one is issued.

## What counts as substantive

A correction is substantive where it changes what a reader would reasonably take an entry to mean. In practice, on this platform, that means:

A misstatement of what an instrument provides, or of the date on which a provision took effect.

A misattribution of a position to a body that does not hold it.

A test guideline number, endpoint, or method description that is wrong.

A statistic altered, withdrawn, or re-sourced.

Anything else — a clearer sentence, a fixed link, a corrected spelling — is a minor correction and is made silently.
