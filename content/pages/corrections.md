We correct errors of fact promptly and visibly. Substantive corrections — those that change the meaning of a field note or a dataset entry — are recorded here permanently. Minor corrections to typography, links, and formatting are made without notice.

To report an error, write to [info@feral-femme.co](mailto:info@feral-femme.co) with the entry, the passage, and the basis for the correction. We would rather be corrected than quoted incorrectly.

This section is not left empty as an oversight. A corrections page with nothing on it either means no substantive errors have been reported, or means errors are not being recorded. We record them here, and this line will be replaced when the first one is issued.

## What counts as substantive

A correction is substantive where it changes what a reader would reasonably take an entry to mean:

A misattribution of a finding, a report, or a quotation to the wrong publisher or body.

A misstatement of what a regulation, ruling, or standard provides, or of the date it took effect.

A statistic altered, withdrawn, or re-sourced.

A company named in the Record Checker against a finding that does not concern it.

Anything else — a clearer sentence, a fixed link, a corrected spelling — is a minor correction and is made silently.
