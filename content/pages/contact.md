## What we cannot do

We cannot tell you whether a specific product is ethical.

Verifying a single brand requires checking its published policy, its certification scope, its market presence, and its ownership — and any answer would be out of date the moment a formulation or a supplier changed.

The [Certification Decoder](/tools/certifications/) and the [Greenwashing Decoder](/tools/greenwashing/) set out the method we would use ourselves. It takes a couple of minutes per brand and produces an answer you can explain, which is more useful than one you have to trust.
