## Corrections

[corrections@feral-femme.co](mailto:corrections@feral-femme.co)

Include the entry, the passage, and the basis for the correction. Substantive corrections are published in full at [/corrections/](/corrections/).

## Press and interviews

[press@feral-femme.co](mailto:press@feral-femme.co)

We are able to comment on regulatory developments, the status of non-animal test methods, and how certification schemes differ. We do not comment on individual brands, and we do not provide quotes endorsing products or organisations.

## Education and licensing

[education@feral-femme.co](mailto:education@feral-femme.co)

For schools, universities, NGOs, and newsrooms wishing to license the curriculum, request slide decks, or commission research. See [/licensing/](/licensing/) for packages.

## What we cannot do

We cannot tell you whether a specific product is cruelty-free. Verifying a single brand's position requires checking its published policy, its certification scope, its market presence, and its ownership — and any answer we gave would be out of date the moment a formulation changed.

[Reading the Label](/learn/reading-the-label/) sets out the method we would use ourselves. It takes about two minutes per brand and produces an answer you can explain, which is more useful than one you have to trust.
