The subject

Two subjects. One machine.

The cruelty is rarely called cruelty. It is called tradition, testing, farming, research, wellness, self-care. We translate the vocabulary, cite the source, and file the receipt — across fashion, food, beauty, wildlife, medicine, tech, and the advertising that obscures all of it.

Written to be verified, not agreed with.

How we work

Public-Record Reporting

Field notes summarise investigations that already exist in the public record — the Sunday Times, the Guardian, Reuters, AP, the New York Times, the Bureau of Investigative Journalism, peer-reviewed studies, parliamentary inquiries, regulatory filings.

Sourced & cited

Editorial Synthesis

Each source is read, reframed in Feral Femme’s restrained editorial voice, and connected to the broader pattern of the industry it documents. The original publisher always gets the credit.

Reframed & attributed

AI-Directed Editorial Imagery

The archive’s visual essays are created using AI image generation, directed by Ayden Rosemary Brown. Each image is a deliberate frame — conceptual editorial work, not documentary photography.

Disclosed throughout
